Frequently asked questions

Local orders are delivered within 1-3 business days. You will receive a updates via email about your item once your order has been shipped.

To start a return or exchange, simply submit your request through our online form or contact the store directly. Then ship the unworn items back to us using the provided prepaid label and we'll process your replacement or refund as soon as it arrives.

If your package is lost or damaged during delivery, please contact the store immediately with your order details. We will do our best to resolve the issue and make sure that you receive your items.

Once we receive your return or exchange request, it typically takes 5-7 business days to process.

Our sizing and fit policy guarantees that items are true to size as described. Offering a hassle-free size exchanges within 30 days for any unworn items with tags attached.
